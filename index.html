About Us

We start with the problem, not the machine.

ERK, short for Enjinia Resolved Krafts, is an engineering and manufacturing company in India. We build custom machines, automation systems and engineered parts, and we work across machining, fabrication, moulding and 3D printing so the process suits the job.

We design every project in 3D first. That way the mechanism, motion and structure are proven before we cut any metal.

What we have built

Machine tending line. Parts are fed in on a conveyor, carried across on a linear axis and presented to the machine, so nobody has to stand at it for the whole cycle.

Retrofit job handling. Added to a machine already running on the floor, so finished parts come out without a hand going into the machine.

Bench welding station. A fixture and indexing head hold every part the same way, so weld quality does not change with whoever is running it.
