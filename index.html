About ERK

An engineering partner for the process your part actually needs

ERK, Enjinia Resolved Krafts, takes an idea, a requirement, or a shop-floor problem, and turns it into a manufactured solution. We are not tied to a single process. Depending on what your part or machine calls for, that can mean CNC machining, 3D printing, injection moulding, sheet metal fabrication, industrial automation, or a custom build engineered from scratch.

Our own track record has been strongest in special purpose machines, automation, and fabrication, and that experience runs through the work shown on this page. As ERK grows, the same engineering discipline is now open to a wider range of manufacturing requirements.
